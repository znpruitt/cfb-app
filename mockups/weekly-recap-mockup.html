Mockup notes (not part of the design) — layout-stage final
  • Decided — Overview zone: full-width tile above the podium accepted; DESIGN.md amendment to document the elevated timely-content zone (draft banner + recap tile). Podium displacement is bounded by the visibility window.
  • Tag color — final assignment folds into INSIGHTS-017-PALETTE: the rendered registry is 8 categories over 5 distinct dark hexes with two collisions already queued for rationalization (HISTORICAL/STANDINGS/SEASON share #AFA9EC; STATS/LEAGUE/fallback share #B4B2A9), and CAREER's #5DCAA5 is allocated but unreachable from the insights path. Assigning tag hues against a palette flagged for rework would be building on sand — instead, 017's scope expands to cover the three game-tag families alongside the insight categories, rationalized as one palette. Sequencing: 017 lands before (or with) the recap enrichment stage; enrichment consumes its tokens. The hues in this mockup (fuchsia/lime/teal) are visual placeholders only. Caution for 017: reclaiming #5DCAA5 sits near delta green #4ade80.
  • Notable results are mini scoreboards: a tag eyebrow with the qualifying stat, then two team lines — team primary, owner as tertiary suffix, score right-anchored. Row order is always away → home (CFB convention), never winner-first; ordering and emphasis are separable, so the winner carries the weight and the loser dims regardless of which line they occupy. Unowned opponents render team-only. The layout replaces the "over" sentence, disambiguates repeat meetings, and reserves the line-start logo slot. Shared micro-component with the live/watchlist scoreboard.
  • Staged rollout: this mockup is the layout target. Stage mapping — layout stage: recap structure, tile + expand-in-place, headline + week records (slice-1 data), sections absent (not placeholdered) until their data slice lands. Wiring stage: leaders, movement, odds-based upsets from existing derivations. Enrichment stage: notable tagging via gameTags.ts extension, threshold constants (BLOWOUT_MARGIN 30, NAILBITER_MARGIN 3, SHOOTOUT_COMBINED 85 — approved), tag color tokens, headline dek richness.
  • Week records line 2 is PF · PA (win% considered and rejected): weekly win% is derived directly from the W–L on line 1, failing DESIGN.md's rule that line-2 metadata be at least as informative as the primary value; and "Win% · Diff" is season-standings vocabulary, inviting a weekly .857 to be read as a season figure. PF · PA is genuinely new information at that position. Comparability across unequal game counts is carried by the comparator-sorted order itself.
  • Zero-results state preserved, restyled: an eligible week with no completed results keeps the shipped state as a single secondary-text line under the recap header ("No completed results were recorded for this week.") — factual, past-tense, no counters, no promise.
  • Thursday cutoff is deliberate: midweek (Tue/Wed) games can start the next week while the recap is still displayed through Wednesday; accepted trade for a fixed calendar rule with no game-data dependency.
  • Resolved earlier: headline leads with best weekly record (league ranks on wins); tie rule reuses the standings comparator (wins → win% → point differential); strongest-tag-wins via existing prioritizeGameTags; week eligibility is calendar-only (06:00 ET the day after the week's last game-date, regardless of game resolution); movement uses poll delta language; compact row proposed as dense sibling token; expansion is client-side, collapsed by default, no duplicated sections in implementation; notables exclude strip-featured games, cap at ~7, render nothing on a quiet week.
  • Decided — scoreboard emphasis is team-primary: team leads the line (primary weight), owner is the tertiary suffix. Rationale: de-conflicts repeat matchups between the same owners, reads as a true scoreboard, and reserves the line-start slot for future team logos — the markup orders team first so the logo insertion point is structural. Logos themselves are out of scope for this campaign.
  • Decided — tile lifecycle (two-state persistent slot): the tile always occupies the elevated zone during the season. State 1, recap: from a week's eligibility (06:00 ET after its last game-date) until Thursday 06:00 ET. State 2, upcoming weekend: Thursday 06:00 ET until the next week's eligibility. One fixed calendar rule, no game-data dependency; state resolved at render. The upcoming-weekend view is NEW SCOPE requiring its own mockup cycle — not designed here, not in this campaign. Until it ships, the tile hides during its window (podium tops the page Thursday–weekend), and likewise at season start before the first eligible week. The incomplete-week impact statement is retired — its window no longer exists.
  • Decided — notable-results scope: all owned-team games qualify for tagging; owner-vs-owner games take selection priority. Concretely: qualifying owner-vs-owner games fill the list first (strongest-tag ordering), then qualifying non-H2H owned games fill remaining slots up to the cap. The scoreboard component renders an unowned opponent as team-only (no owner suffix) — see the Purdue example in the expanded notables.
  • Dek is templated multi-fact prose, specced as such: the enrichment-stage spec must define the composition template and degradation rules (no close game, no movers, single dominant owner) — not "richness" hand-waving. The upset detail requires spread magnitude, which the shared gameTags.ts helper already returns.
